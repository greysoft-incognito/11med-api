## What this covers

This explains what 11med does with your health information, who can see it, and what you can do about it. It is written to be read, not to be survived.

11med is operated by [registered company name]. We handle personal and health data under the Nigeria Data Protection Act.

## What we collect

To create your account: your name, phone number, and a password. An email address if you choose to add one.

To build your medical profile: date of birth, gender, blood type, genotype, your state and local government area, drug allergies, ongoing conditions, an emergency contact, and health insurance details if you add them.

To run your medication reminders: the medicines you add, their doses and times, and whether each dose was taken, skipped, snoozed or missed.

We do not collect your location, your contacts, or anything from your device beyond what you type in.

## Why we hold it

Your medication schedule exists so the app can remind you at the right time and work out how consistently you are taking your treatment.

Your medical profile exists so it can be shown to a clinician when it matters, including through your emergency QR code.

We do not use your health data for advertising, and we do not sell it. Not to anyone, in any form.

## Who can see it

You, on your own devices.

Any caregiver you personally invite. They can see your medication schedule and how you are doing with it. They cannot change anything, and you can remove their access at any time.

Anyone who scans your emergency QR code. That is the point of it: it is designed to be readable at a hospital by someone who has never met you. It shows a deliberately limited set of facts, blood type, genotype, drug allergies, ongoing conditions, current medications and your emergency contact, and never your full history. You can revoke the code whenever you want, which makes every copy of it stop working.

Nobody else, unless the law requires it of us.

## Where it is kept

On your phone, so the app works when you have no signal. Your schedule and profile are cached on the device, and the credential that keeps you signed in is held in the phone's own encrypted storage rather than in ordinary app storage.

On our servers, so it survives losing your phone and can reach a second device.

Signing out removes the cached copy from that device, along with any scheduled reminders and any dose answers waiting to be sent.

## Your rights

You can see everything we hold about you, from inside the app.

You can correct any of it, from the same place.

You can delete your account from Settings. It removes your profile, your medications, your dose history, your notifications and your caregiver links. It is immediate and it cannot be undone.

You can withdraw consent by deleting your account. There is no version of 11med that works without holding your health data, so we will not pretend otherwise.

If you want to raise something, contact us at [privacy contact email]. You also have the right to complain to the Nigeria Data Protection Commission.

## How long we keep it

While your account exists. After deletion we remove your record, except anything we are legally required to retain, which we hold for [retention period] and use for nothing else.
