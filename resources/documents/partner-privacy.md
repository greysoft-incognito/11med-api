## What we hold about you

For the business: its name, licence, address, contact details and what it lists. For each member of staff: their name, email address, password (stored only as a one-way hash), the devices they signed in on, and the actions they took in the portal.

## Why we hold it

To list your business to patients, pass you their orders and bookings, pay you, keep your accounts secure, and answer you when you contact support.

## Who can see it

Patients see your listing. The 11med team sees what it needs to approve, support and pay you, and its access is recorded. Paystack receives what it needs to take and send payments. Nothing is sold to anyone.

## Patients’ information

Patient details you see in the portal are health information under the Nigeria Data Protection Act. They are shown to you for one order or booking and must be handled as the terms of service set out.

## How long we keep it

For as long as your business is listed, then for [retention period] where the law requires it. A member of staff you remove keeps no access from that moment.

## Your rights

You may ask to see, correct or remove what we hold about you, within what the law requires us to keep. Write to [privacy contact email].
