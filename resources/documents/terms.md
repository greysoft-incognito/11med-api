## What 11med is

11med helps you keep track of your medication and hold your health record in one place. It reminds you when a dose is due and records what you took.

## What 11med is not

It is not medical advice, and it is not a doctor. It does not know whether a medicine is right for you, whether a dose is safe, or what to do if you feel unwell.

It will warn you if a medicine you add matches an allergy you have recorded. That check is only as good as what you have entered, and it is not a substitute for a pharmacist or a doctor reading your prescription.

If something is wrong with your health, contact a medical professional. Do not wait for the app.

## Reminders are best effort

Reminders are scheduled on your phone. They depend on your phone being on, having battery, and having notifications turned on for 11med. A phone that is off does not remind you.

Do not rely on 11med as the only thing standing between you and a missed dose of something critical.

## Your account

Keep your password to yourself. Anyone who can sign in as you can read your medical record.

Your phone number identifies your account and is how you recover it. Keep it current with your network.

You are responsible for what you enter. If you record the wrong dose, the app will remind you to take the wrong dose.

## Caregivers

You choose who can see your medication. A caregiver can only look; they cannot add, change or remove anything.

Removing a caregiver takes effect immediately.

## Paid features

Some features are limited on the free plan. Those limits are shown wherever they apply, and your existing data always remains yours to read and export regardless of plan.

Subscriptions are not sold inside the app.
