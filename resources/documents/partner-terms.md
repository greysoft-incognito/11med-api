## Who these cover

These terms apply to a licensed pharmacy or laboratory listed on 11med, and to every member of its staff who signs in to the portal. The account owner accepts them for the business; each member of staff accepts them when they sign in.

## Your listing

You keep your licence current and tell us when it changes. What you list (medicines, tests, prices, stock, collection terms and pictures) must be accurate, and pictures must show the real product packaging. The 11med team may suspend a listing that is not, and will tell you why.

## Orders and bookings

A patient pays before an order or booking reaches you, except a pickup they pay for at your counter. You move each one forward only when that step has happened, because the patient sees every step you record. A cancellation refunds the patient in full.

Prescription-only medicines are sold only against a prescription issued through 11med.

## Patient information

You see a patient’s details only to fulfil their order or booking. You may not copy them out of the portal, contact the patient for anything else, or share them. Laboratory results are uploaded as the document you wrote and are seen by the patient alone.

## Your staff

The account owner decides who on your staff has an account and removes those who leave. You are responsible for what is done under your accounts. Keep passwords private and use two-step verification where you can.
